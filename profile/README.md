We are the Energy System Analysis (ESA) research group at the [Institute for Automation and Applied Informatics (IAI)](https://www.iai.kit.edu/english/index.php) of the [Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT)](https://www.kit.edu/english/). We study how electricity, gas and heat networks work together and how they interact with buildings and transport. For this work we build software and models.

### Software

Our software suite is [eASiMOV](https://easimov.com), short for Energy System Analysis, Simulation, Modeling, Optimization and Visualization. It covers co-simulation, power grids, heat networks, buildings and data services.

Some of our tools are already open source in the [KIT-IAI](https://github.com/KIT-IAI) organization: [ePowCoRe](https://github.com/KIT-IAI/ePowCoRe), [Copy-Paste Imputation](https://github.com/KIT-IAI/CopyPasteImputation) and [PyAPI-RTS](https://github.com/KIT-IAI/PyAPI-RTS).
